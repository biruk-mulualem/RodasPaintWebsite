                                                <span style="color: black; font-weight: bolder;">Application:</span> 
                                                <ul>
                                                <li>Apply RODAS LINOL using the special brush in an oblique direction from the top to the bottom alternating the strokes from right to left in the same direction. Wait 10-15 minutes until the smooth parts are dried, rough is still wet. Smooth the surface with the special spatula following the used direction. After 4-5 hours apply another coat with the same technique following the same direction, with another direction you can obtain another effect.  </li>
                                                <li> Rodas LINOL METALLIC is the metallic version that is ready to use in combination with the desired colored base coat. </li>
                                                <li> Rodas LINOL SPECIAL is the brilliant version which is a combination of several colored base coats and different options. </li>
                                                <li> Rodas LINOL MASPERLATO is the pearled version which is obtained by applying a colored based coat top coated by the white flakes (RODAS LINOL WHITE) and finally with a layer of the pearled coat (RODAS LINOL MASPERLATO) choosing from the combinations offered by the catalog. </li>
                                                <li>Rodas LINOL MULTI has multicolor flakes and is ready to use.  </li>
                                                </ul>
                                                  <span style="color: black; font-weight: bolder;">Coverage:</span> 8-10 m2/Ltr. 2 coats are suggested.
