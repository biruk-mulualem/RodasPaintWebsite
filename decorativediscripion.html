Rodas ALBA Description

General Description

ALBA is a water-based wall decorative paint with special pearlescent pigment intended for internal application. It creates metallic finishes with deep elegance, is odorless, non–toxic, and washable.

Advantages

  • Easy application
  • Low odor and low VOC level
  • Ready to use
  • Can be tinted with various colors

Characteristics & Physical Properties

Appearance Metallic
Grades Smooth, Fine, Medium, Coarse
Specific Weight 1.0 Kg/Ltr
Method of Application Brush, Roller, Spatula, Leather cloth, Trowel
Coverage 8–12 m²/Kg depending on surface and application
Drying Time 3–4 Hours
Dilution 5–10% with fresh water
VOC 52 g/Ltr

Surface Preparation

New Concrete:

  • Fully cured (28 days minimum), clean, and contaminant-free
  • Prime with Masonry AR Primer, Masonry Primer, PVA Wall filler, sanding, and intermediate coat

Old Concrete:

  • Remove blisters, cracks, and loose paint
  • Wash with fungicidal solution if necessary
  • Sand glossy finish coats

Method of Application

  • Prepare surface for painting
  • Apply ALBA with trowel in vertical/horizontal strokes
  • Finish with desired texture using brush, roller, trowel, or leather cloth

Storage & Packing

Shelf life: 12 months (below 25°C, frost-free)

Keep sealed, away from heat & sunlight

Available in 1 L, 4 Kg, 10 Kg

Physiological Hazards

Non-toxic, but ensure good ventilation

Harmful if swallowed or in contact with eyes

Rodas AQUA METALIC Description

General Description

Rodas Aqua Metallic is a water-based paint based on vinyl versatate copolymer with luster pigment that gives a brilliant metallic decorative finish. It is odorless, non-toxic, and washable, ideal to use on gypsum, cornices, frames for antique finishes, and a top coat for steel decoration.

Advantages

  • Easy application
  • Low odor and low VOC level
  • Ready to use
  • It can be tinted with a variety of desired colors

Characteristics and Physical Properties

Appearance Metallic
Sp. Weight 1.00 Kg/Ltr.
Method of Application Brush, Roller, Spatula, Leather cloth, Trowel
Coverage 6-8 m²/Kg depending on the type of application.
Flash point Non-flammable
Dilution Fresh water if required
VOC 75 g/Ltr.

Surface Preparation

New Concrete:

  • The substrate must be primed by Rodas Masonry primer, putty filler sanding, and intermediate coat with mascryl (any color) steel.
  • Surface to be coated must be degreased and de-rusted. It must be clean and dry prior to the application of an appropriate primer.

Steel:

  • Apply Rodas Aqua Metallic by brush, roller or spray.

Storage:

  • Shelf life 12 months minimum if stored below 25°C.

Cleaning of Tools:

  • All tools should be cleaned with water as soon as possible.

Packaging:

  • Available in 1/2kg, 1kg, and 5kg.

Method of Application

There are so many ways of applying Rodas Aqua Metallic by normal brush, roller sponge, spatula, leather cloth, and spray gun.

Rodas Aqua Metallic can be overcoated with a contrast color of the same material to give a unique design.

Physiological Hazards

Rodas Aqua Metallic does not contain solvents, nevertheless, good ventilation in working rooms is recommended as well as the use of safety tools and equipment. Rodas Aqua Metallic is harmful if swallowed. If contact with eyes or skin occurs, wash well with freshwater and seek immediate medical advice.

Rodas DAMASCO Description

General Description

Rodas DAMASCO is decorative covering for interior walls based on special natural raw materials giving an antique look, achieving this original RODAS DAMASCO effect, which is especially suitable for classical as well as for modern style decoration. The product is given a high level of breathability, prevents mildew and fungi growth, and environment friendly.

Characteristics and Physical Properties

Viscosity Pasty
Gloss Eggshell
Theo. yield 150 ~ 200 g/m2
Shelf life 12 months
VOC 75 g/Ltr.

Storage

  • The product must be stored in accordance with national requisitions. The product should be kept in a cool and well-ventilated place, protected from heat direct sunlight, containers must be kept tightly closed.

Packing

  • Rodas DAMASCO is available in 1kg, 4kg, and 20 kg PAIL.

Surface Preparation

  • For the new concrete, it should be fully cured at least 28 days and the substrate must be free from any contaminants.
  • If old concrete, all blistered, cracks, and peeled off paint should be removed.

Typical Recommended System:

  • Typical Recommended System.
  • Level the surface with PVA Wall Filler if necessary and sand until smooth
  • Apply 1 – 2 coats of Masonry Primer tinted with the desired color.
  • Apply 1 – 2 coats of RODAS DAMASCO tinted and diluted with 30% water as a base coat.
  • Apply the effect coat using the explained tools and technique.

Safety Measures

  • Use in a well-ventilated area.
  • Wear gloves to avoid skin contact.
  • Avoid contact with eyes; rinse with water if needed.
  • Keep out of reach of children.

Rodas LINOL METALLIC Description


General Description

Rodas LINOL METALLIC is a water-based and transpiring decorative paint with particular and beautiful stained, antique and classic effects. The easy application helps to redecorate houses, villas, shops, flats, etc.

Application:
  • Apply RODAS LINOL using the special brush in an oblique direction from top to bottom alternating strokes from right to left in the same direction. Wait 10-15 minutes until smooth parts are dry, rough parts still wet. Smooth the surface with the special spatula following the used direction. After 4-5 hours, apply another coat with the same technique following the same direction. Changing direction creates different effects.
  • Rodas LINOL METALLIC is the metallic version ready to use in combination with the desired colored base coat.
  • Rodas LINOL SPECIAL is the brilliant version, a combination of several colored base coats and different options.
  • Rodas LINOL MASPERLATO is the pearled version, obtained by applying a colored base coat topped by white flakes (RODAS LINOL WHITE) and finally a layer of the pearled coat (RODAS LINOL MASPERLATO), choosing from catalog combinations.
  • Rodas LINOL MULTI has multicolor flakes and is ready to use.

Coverage: 8-10 m²/Ltr. Two coats are suggested.

Dilution: Ready to use.

Tool Cleaning: Water.

Storage: The product should be kept in a cool and well-ventilated place, protected from heat and direct sunlight. Containers must be tightly closed. The product complies with EU legislation on VOC limits.
