Rodas ALBA Description

General Description

ALBA is a water-based wall decorative paint with special pearlescent pigment intended for internal application. It creates metallic finishes with deep elegance, is odorless, non–toxic, and washable.

Characteristics & Physical Properties

Appearance Metallic
Grades Smooth, Fine, Medium, Coarse
Specific Weight 1.0 Kg/Ltr
Method of Application Brush, Roller, Spatula, Leather cloth, Trowel
Coverage 8–12 m²/Kg depending on surface and application
Drying Time 3–4 Hours
Dilution 5–10% with fresh water
VOC 52 g/Ltr

Advantages

  • Easy application
  • Low odor and low VOC level
  • Ready to use
  • Can be tinted with various colors

Surface Preparation

New Concrete:

  • Fully cured (28 days minimum), clean, and contaminant-free
  • Prime with Masonry AR Primer, Masonry Primer, PVA Wall filler, sanding, and intermediate coat

Old Concrete:

  • Remove blisters, cracks, and loose paint
  • Wash with fungicidal solution if necessary
  • Sand glossy finish coats

Method of Application

  • Prepare surface for painting
  • Apply ALBA with trowel in vertical/horizontal strokes
  • Finish with desired texture using brush, roller, trowel, or leather cloth

Storage & Packing

Shelf life: 12 months (below 25°C, frost-free)

Keep sealed, away from heat & sunlight

Available in 1 L, 4 Kg, 10 Kg

Physiological Hazards

Non-toxic, but ensure good ventilation

Harmful if swallowed or in contact with eyes

Rodas MARMORINO Description


General Description

Rodas MARMORINO is based on the 12th-century technique, used by the Venetian’s to incorporate art into building design. It has been developed from mineral lime, marble dust, natural pigment plus additives, and modernized to suit today’s design without losing the quality of the traditional product. Although today’s designs can dictate color and texture, the basic formula has been retained to create a spectrum of color ranging from natural stone to a marble-like antique design.

Characteristics and Physical Properties

Contains Natural mineral lime
Viscosity 2000-3500 cps at 25°C
Appearance Paste
Fire Rating 0 (Non-flammable / Non-combustible)
Sp. Weight 1.5-1.7 Kg/Ltr.
VOC 48 g/Ltr.
Plaster 0.9–1.0 Kg/m²
Gypsum 0.5–0.8 Kg/m²
Touch Dry 1-2 Hrs.
Recoat Ability 6-12 Hrs.
Pencil Hardness 5 Hrs.
Dilution Water
    Product Range:
  • Marmorino Smooth
  • Marmorino Paste Medium
  • Marmorino Rough
  • Marmorino Classic
  • Marmorino Natural
  • Marmorino Shells
  • Marmosilk

Lightweight surface, maximum 3mm thick enable architects to make considerable cost savings on basic construction costs. Each finish is individually designed to suit the tastes and requirements of the designers.

Smooth: Plain colored, very smooth with a good sheet. The material is fine marble dust that is applied in a paste form, can be applied up to 3 layers.

Travertine: Use larger marble aggregate and is therefore much tougher. It can be blended with various pigments to create almost any color. It provides a finish with a porous, line effect that recalls the typical look of travertine marble.

Pitted: Rugged and more visible grain effect or a less polished surface to create and feel honed limestone. It is effective when used to create the appearance of monolithic natural stone.

Dragged: It is another pattern to recreate the same material for pitted and travertine to achieve a grainy yet semi-polished top running to the direction of the required stroke.

Metallic: Metallic finishes can be achieved by using metallic pigments to be mixed with Rodas MARMORINO.

Suitable substrates

  • Rodas MARMORINO Decorative plaster can be applied to the most solid surfaces. Make sure that the surface is thoroughly dried and aged. Resurface with the specific products if necessary. Cracks should be filled with suitable crack filler or similar products. In case, the surface is flaky, apply Masonry AR Primer to avoid efflorescence.
  • Dry lined surfaces (plasterboard) should be double boarded with staggered joints. Joints in MDF surfaces should be taped.
  • Raw substrates such as concrete blockwork, brickwork and similar backgrounds must be rendered with a base plaster to achieve a true, flat surface that is straight both horizontally and vertically.
  • All supports should be sound, clean, dry and free of dust and greasy matter. In hot weather, the support should be moistened.
  • All substrates should be prepared as if for final decoration.
  • It is not recommended to apply over wallpaper or lining paper. Extra care should be taken on surfaces that are previously decorated.

Application Method

    All surfaces are treated with a tie – coat of ACRITEX PRIMER or BOND PRIMER to ensure good adhesion of the product to the background surface.

  • Rodas MARMORINO are ready to use the material, dilute with water if required then applied in a number of thin layers to build a thickness of 1-2 mm.

Surface Preparation

New Concrete:

The surface must be clean, structurally sound, and free from all contaminants including curing compounds, form release agents, dust, dirt, oil, old coatings, and paint. Old Concrete:

Old concrete should be clean and in good state of repair i.e., cracks, holes, and other irregularities should be filled in. All blistered, cracks and peeled-off paints should be removed either by grit blasting, mechanical sanding, or paint stripper. Penetrated paint must be cleaned and treated with an ammonia solution. Glossy finish coat must be sanded. Oils and greases are best removed by steam cleaning in conjunction with a detergent solution. Thorough scrubbing with hot caustic solutions will also remove mineral oils and greases.

Application Procedure:

The product can be applied with the aid of a stainless steel trowel. Prime the substrate with Rodas Masonry Primer or Primary on very absorbent and chalky surfaces. In the case of a flaky surface, the application of Primkote is a must. If the surface does not require any fixative, it is nonetheless recommended to dampen it to prevent the wall from drawing an excessive amount of water from the binder. At least two (2) coats of the product must be applied to prepare the undercoat. Smooth the surface by stainless steel spatula using Rodas Stucco Grassello to get a uniform finish. After 6 hours from the last coat, sand with sandpaper in order to smooth the surface and take off surface defects. Apply the last coat of Rodas Stucco Grassello by stainless steel spatula thoroughly. To obtain a different effect, choose a different size of a spatula, and apply artistically. After 5 – 10 minutes press the spatula and move it on the surface to obtain more glossy effects. Since the drying and carbonation of Stucco Grassello are highly susceptible to the temperature and humidity levels, it is advisable to apply the product when temperatures are in the range of +100C to 350C and the relative humidity of the air must be less than 80%.
