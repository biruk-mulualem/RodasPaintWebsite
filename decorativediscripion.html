Rodas TILAL Description

Download PDF

General Description

Rodas TILAL is a water-based decorative paint for interior and exterior use that renders professional results with simple application convenience. Unlike traditional flux finishes, Rodas TILAL does not require intermixing of colors, layering techniques, or sophisticated equipment. Simple effortless trowelling is all that is required to achieve this exquisite effect. Due to its natural mineral blend, it provides a Marmorino effect that gives an elegant monochromatic finish and is environment friendly.

Major Uses: Can be applied onto walls, columns, and other curved surfaces leaving a joint-free finish. For exterior applications, a protective coat of Rodas PROTECT is required to ensure a long-lasting effect.

Characteristics and Physical Properties

Viscosity Paste
Sp. Weight 1.50 Kg/Ltr.
Yield 750-1000 g/m²
Touch Dry 1–2 Hrs.
Gloss Matt
Shelf Life 12 Months
Application Trowel
Dilution Not required
VOC 4.0 g/Ltr.

Surface Preparation

Allow new masonry to dry for 14 days under normal weather conditions before painting. Treat new concrete with a neutralizer and let dry overnight before rinsing with water to remove white crystals (efflorescence). Let dry before painting. Surfaces should be free from oil, grease, dust, dirt, contaminants, and all loose grit and mortar.

If repainting, remove all loose paint, scaling, flaking, and peeling paint by PAINT REMOVER, rinse, and let dry. Wooden surfaces should be sanded smooth to the touch with no rough edges. Dust off the surface, then wipe with a clean rag. Prime concrete with Rodas Masonry Primer (interior), Rodas Acrylic Prime White (exterior), and Rodas Undercoat Enamel for wooden surfaces. After primer application, apply Rodas TILAL by trowel uniformly to fully cover the surfaces. For a double color effect, apply another coat of the desired tone by trowel and scrape with a spatula to enhance the design.

Storage and Handling

The product must be stored in accordance with national regulations. It should be kept in a cool and well-ventilated place, protected from heat and direct sunlight. Containers must be tightly closed.

Packing: Rodas TILAL is available in 5Kg (4Kg base + 1Kg Rodas AD pre-weighed bucket) and 25Kg (20Kg base + 5Kg Rodas AD pre-weighed pail).

Physiological Hazards: Rodas TILAL does not contain solvents; however, good ventilation in working areas and the use of safety equipment are recommended. It is harmful if swallowed. In case of contact with eyes or skin, wash well with fresh water and seek immediate medical advice.

Precautions: See Safety Indication Sheet.

Rodas MICROCEM Description

Download PDF

General Description

Rodas MICROCEM is a high-quality two-component multipurpose decorative and resurfacing material based on a special binder and natural limestone to decorate floors and walls. It is recommended for interior and exterior use but is most useful in redecorating interior floors and walls with a choice of different colors.

Major Uses

Rodas MICROCEM can be applied to well-prepared substrates such as walls and floors. It adheres firmly to concrete, overlays, tiles, ceramics, and other cementitious surfaces.

Mixing and Application Method

Basecoat:

Rodas MICROCEM Base should be mixed with a forced action mechanical mixer or a heavy-duty drill fitted with a purpose-made paddle.

Mix Rodas MICROCEM Base B with the desired color into the mixing vessel and slowly add Rodas MICROCEM Base A in accordance with the required mixing until a smooth consistency is obtained. Mix for a minimum of three minutes. If the mixture is too dense, add water until you reach the desired consistency.

Rodas MICROCEM may be applied by stainless steel trowel at a rate of approximately 1.5 m²/kg.

Scrape over the layered material to remove any small lumps or trowel lines. Pay attention to the corners to avoid an uneven finish.

Leave the first layer to dry to touch for about an hour depending on the temperature of the working place.

Repeat the process progressively over the wall, occasionally passing over the area already coated to achieve the required design and effect.

Keep on trowelling the surface with a cleaned stainless steel trowel for about 15-20 minutes until the surface is smooth.

Topcoat:

When the basecoat is well dry, sand the surface with 220 grit sandpaper. Remove all laitance and dust by vacuum cleaner prior to the application of Rodas MICROCEM Topcoat.

Repeat the same procedure as the basecoat. Apply the topcoat at a rate of 4 m²/kg.

Leave the surface to dry for about 2 days before application of subsequent coating.
