INTRODUCTION

Super Double T General Trading Plc was established as a Private Limited Company business entity in 1999 E.C and as a holding company, there are several business entities under its name. The company engaged in different business activities namely manufacturing, import, contracting wholesales and retail sales.

Super Fiber Glass Products manufacturing, Rodas Paint Industry, Waterproofing Products works, Solar Energy Products technology, Super Rubber Seal Products and Marble Products are the main sectors under Super Double “T” General Trading plc.

The diversification of our products, manufacturing of marketable products, wholesales and distribution of imported items is based on the detail market research and experience on the business. The company increases its performance from time to time by additional advantages of incorporating human resources, market assessments, and the capability of selecting niche markets.

Our major asset is the human resource of the company, inspiration, unstinted efforts backed up with extensive experiences acquired in various sectors of the business world, which therefore makes out to be the invaluable resource of the organization. Super Double T General Trading PLC makes sure that all the members of the company managements are always dedicated to contributing their own knowledge for the growth of the company, through applying their vast experience in private business organizations, government organizations, public enterprise, non-government organizations including international organizations at different capacities and managerial levels.

MISSION

Our mission is to stand by our customers and the business environment as a whole in provision of supplying quality products which are the result of the technology for the consumers.

VISION

Our vision is to realize modern corporate international business holdings of well-known brands of consumable and commercial products in Ethiopia in particular and East Africa in general.

GOAL

Our goal is to make our company the leader in market share for Rodas Paint, maintain our market share for Super Fiber Glass product, and ensure sustainable growth of our company.

HUMAN RESOURCE & MANAGEMENT PROFILE

We prioritize working closely with our clients, gathering feedback, and maintaining a readiness to take swift action. Whether handling large-scale projects or smaller assignments, we have the flexibility to adjust our capacity to meet our clients' needs and respond to market demands effectively.

Keys to Success

The key to our success lies in several fundamental factors, including an unshakable organization and management structure that ensures stability and growth. The Difference
