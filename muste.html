Rodas Alba Description


General Description

ALBA is a water-based wall decorative paint with special pearlescent pigment intended for internal application. It creates many different metallic finishes with deep surface elegance. It is odorless, non–toxic, and washable.


Download the specification in PDF

Advantages

  • Easy application
  • Low odor and low VOC level
  • Ready to use
  • It can be tinted with a variety of desired colors

Characteristics and Physical Properties

Color
Appearance Metallic
Grades Smooth, Fine, Medium, Coarse
Sp. Weight 1.0 Kg/Ltr.
Method of Application Brush, Roller, Spatula, Leather cloth, Trowel
Coverage 8–12 m²/Kg. depending on the porosity of the surface and type of application.
Drying time 3–4 Hours
Dilution 5–10% with fresh water
VOC 52 g/Ltr.

Surface Preparation

New Concrete:
  • It should be fully cured at least 28 days and the substrate must be free from any form of contaminants.
  • Ensure that the surface to be coated is sound and clean.
  • The substrate must be primed by Masonry AR Primer, Masonry Primer, PVA Wall filler, sanding, and intermediate coat with cryl (any color) or decorative basecoat.
Old Concrete:
  • All blisters, cracks and peeled off paint should be removed.
  • Remove any loose or flaking material back to a sound firm edge.
  • Treat any organic growth with a fungicidal wash.
  • Penetrated paint must be cleared and treated with 3% ammonia and water.
  • Glossy finish coat must be sanded.
Method of Application:
  • Surface to be coated must be prepared as ready for painting.
  • Apply ALBA to the surface with a trowel both vertically and horizontally to achieve a consistent. Work in 1–2 m² sections at a time. For a smoother finish, lightly run a smoothing spatula across the surface to remove any stipple.
  • At the edges (architrave, picture rail, and skirting) or corners, apply the ALBA with a paintbrush and smoothen with a trowel or spatula.
  • Leave to dry for about 3–4 hours under drying condition and lightly sand the surface with sand, not to the extent of destroying the effect of the paint.
  • Apply the final layer of the paint to the surface with the desired design using either Brush, Roller, Trowel, Leather cloth as thin as possible and work with the chosen tools in a motion that will create a unique design.

Storage and Packing

Shelf life 12 months minimum if stored below 25°C. Protect from frost

Protect from the source of sunlight and direct heat. Keep container sealed when not in use.

Available in 1 Liter, 4 Kg, 10 Kg.

Physiological Hazards

ALBA is nontoxic, nevertheless, good ventilation in working rooms is recommended as well as the use of safety tools and equipment.

It is harmful if swallowed or contacted with eye

Rodas GALAXY Description


General Description

Rodas GALAXY is a water-based decorative paint reminding the spectacular effect of outer space galaxies. It is easy to apply and available in a wide range of colors. This paint is very resistant and washable.


Download the specification in PDF

Characteristics and Physical Properties

Composition Acrylic resins, Special pigments and Glitters
Diluents Water
Theo. Yield 5 - 8 m²/Ltr.
Appearance Satin matt
Gloss Eggshell
Dilution 5–10% with fresh water
VOC 80 g/Ltr.
Storage Keep in a closed container at room temperature.
Washing Resistance Washes well with water or common detergent
Application By brush, roller or trowel

Surface Preparation

NEW CONCRETE WALLS:
  • the wall should be fully dry without alkaline substance and it should be smooth. Otherwise, scrape with PVA Wall filler, then sand with 180 grit sandpaper. Apply one (1) coat of MAS Masonry Primer White or Mascryl prior to the application of Galaxy
NEW WOOD:
  • application of PVA Wall filler (if necessary), then sand with 280 – grit sandpaper until a smooth surface is obtained. Apply one (1) coat of RODAS Undercoat White and two (2) coats of Galaxy with an interval of 5 – 6 hours at room temperature with Smoothen with 280 – 300 grit sandpaper to facilitate the adhesion of Primary 300 and then the Galaxy.
NEW IRON:
  • smoothen the surface with sandpaper. Remove traces of oil, grease, and other contaminants and apply one (1) coat of Deruster. Apply one (1) cot of Primary 300 prior to the application of two (2) coats of Galaxy.
LIGHT ALLOYS:
  • sremove grease and smooth the surface and apply a coat of adequate primer. Apply one (1) coat of Primary 300 before the application of Galaxy.
Method of Application:
  • To obtain the best effect and quality results, we suggest the following application recommendations. To attain an excellent finish, it is advisable to apply a primer coat. It can be applied on various surfaces like concrete walls, plaster, wood panels, iron ad-light alloys, etc. after previous preparation

Technical Features

Application & Drying:Apply RODAS GALAXY between 50°C and 400°C. Dries to touch in 1 hour at 20°C and fully cures in 3–24 hours depending on conditions.

Durability & Cleaning:Once dry, the coating is abrasion-resistant and resists alcohol, water, and common soaps. Clean tools immediately with water.

Storage & Packaging:Store in a cool, ventilated place away from heat and sunlight. Available in 5 kg and 25 kg packs.

NOTE

To obtain obvious and shining glitter, allow the paint to dry for about 24 hours. Soak a sponge on clean water and try to rinse the paint carefully until all glitters will be visible.

The color in the catalog serves only as guidelines and we cannot guarantee the perfect reproduction of the colors.

Durability & Cleaning:Once dry, the coating is abrasion-resistant and resists alcohol, water, and common soaps. Clean tools immediately with water.

Storage & Packaging:Store in a cool, ventilated place away from heat and sunlight. Available in 5 kg and 25 kg packs.

NOTE

To obtain obvious and shining glitter, allow the paint to dry for about 24 hours. Soak a sponge on clean water and try to rinse the paint carefully until all glitters will be visible.
