Super Koki Waterproofing

Super Koki, a brand under Super Double "T" General Trading PLC, is the leading waterproof engineering company in the country. We offer top-notch waterproofing solutions to protect your property from water damage, ensuring long-lasting results and structural integrity.

What is Waterproofing?
Waterproofing is the process of making a structure resistant to water, preventing moisture from entering and causing damage. This is achieved through membranes and coatings, which safeguard the contents inside and protect the building's structural strength.



Our Services

We offer cementitious waterproofing with epoxy for internal surfaces and membrane waterproofing with polymer-modified asphalt for external walls, ensuring durable protection against water infiltration and environmental damage.

Our Process

We begin by repairing any cracks and holes to create a smooth, even surface. Once the surface is properly prepared, our expert technicians apply high-quality waterproofing membranes to ensure long-lasting protection.

Warranty

We provide a 10-year warranty on all new waterproofing installations, ensuring your peace of mind for years to come. Additionally, our manufacturers offer a 10-year product warranty,
